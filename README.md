
# ✝️ DailyGrace

### A moment with God's Word.

DailyGrace is a peaceful Windows desktop application designed to bring God's Word into your daily life through Bible verses, atmospheric backgrounds, and a clean, modern interface.

Take a moment to pause, reflect, and reconnect with your faith.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📖 **Daily Bible Verses** — Discover different Bible verses automatically.
- 🌍 **Multilingual Support** — Verses change according to your Windows display language.
- 🎬 **Video Backgrounds** — Create a peaceful atmosphere with custom MP4 backgrounds.
- 🌫️ **Blur Effects** — Adjust the background blur to your preference.
- 🎵 **Background Music** — Listen to ambient music while reading.
- 🎨 **Modern Interface** — Minimalist dark design with animated gray and white borders.
- ⚙️ **Settings Panel** — Customize blur intensity, music, and verse intervals.
- 🪟 **Windows Startup** — Optionally launch DailyGrace when you sign in to Windows.
- 💾 **Saved Preferences** — Your settings are stored between sessions.
- 🎞️ **Smooth Animations** — Enjoy fade transitions between Bible verses.

## ⚙️ Customization

Open the settings panel to customize your experience:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| Background Blur | Adjust the blur intensity. |
| Background Music | Enable or disable ambient music. |
| Verse Interval | Choose how frequently verses change. |
| Windows Startup | Enable or disable automatic startup. |

You can also replace `background.mp4` and `music.mp3` with your own media files.

---

## 🌍 Supported Languages

DailyGrace currently includes verse collections for:

- 🇵🇹 Portuguese
- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇪🇸 Spanish
- 🇫🇷 French
- 🇩🇪 German
- 🇮🇹 Italian

The application selects a language based on the Windows display language, with English as the fallback.
